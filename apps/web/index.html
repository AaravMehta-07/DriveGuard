Features

Everything you need to drive confidently

DriveGuard combines familiar turn-by-turn navigation with real-time compliance intelligence, so you can focus on driving while we watch for restrictions.

📍

Full Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Search, route, and navigate just like Google Maps. Destination search, route alternatives, live traffic, rerouting — everything you expect.

📷

Enforcement Camera Map

See verified speed cameras and signal enforcement points plotted on the map. Browse the enforcement explorer anytime, even without navigating.

⚡

Speed Limit Awareness

Always see your current speed and the applicable speed limit. Get warned when you're over — even when no camera is nearby.

🚫

Illegal Turn Prevention

DriveGuard warns you before prohibited left turns, right turns, U-turns, and no-entry roads. Never get a challan for an innocent-looking turn.

🛡

Copilot Mode

Start DriveGuard without a destination. It follows your drive, showing speed limits, cameras, and restrictions in the background — even while using another map app.

📊

Route Intelligence

Before you start, see how many cameras, signal enforcement points, and restrictions are on your route. Know what to expect before you drive.

🛡

Built for Safety, Not for Cheating

DriveGuard is not an app for defeating enforcement. We encourage you to follow road rules throughout your journey. Camera warnings supplement continuous speed-limit compliance. Our goal is to help you drive legally and confidently — not to help you speed between cameras.

DriveGuard provides navigation and road-compliance assistance using available data. Road conditions and restrictions can change. Always follow posted traffic signs, signals, and directions from authorities. DriveGuard does not guarantee freedom from challans.
